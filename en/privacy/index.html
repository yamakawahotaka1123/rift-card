Privacy policy

This is a translation of the Japanese original. If the two differ, the Japanese version takes precedence.

SUMMONER CARD (“the Site”) handles user information as follows.

1. Your input and images

What you enter on the card (summoner name, contact details and so on) and the images you upload are processed only in your browser. They are never sent to or stored on our server.

So you can pick up where you left off, your input is saved in your browser’s storage (localStorage). This data exists only on your device and is erased when you delete the site data in your browser or press “Reset everything” in the tool.

2. Analytics

To improve the Site, we use Google Analytics, an analytics tool by Google LLC. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect page views, referrers, device types and how often the Site’s features are used (such as how many images are saved and which themes are chosen). This data is aggregated anonymously and does not identify individuals. The text you enter and your images themselves are not sent.

You can refuse this data collection by disabling cookies in your browser or by using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. For how Google handles data, see Google’s Privacy & Terms.

3. Advertising

To cover running costs, the Site may in the future use third-party advertising services (such as Google AdSense). In that case, ad providers may use cookies to show ads based on your interests. We will update this policy before introducing ads.

4. External services

The Site loads data from the following external services in order to display content. When it does, connection information such as your device’s IP address is sent to each service.

  • Riot Games “Data Dragon” (champion, icon and skin images)
  • Community Dragon (rank emblems, role icons and summoner icon images)
  • Google Fonts (web fonts)
  • GitHub Pages (hosting of the Site)

5. Disclaimer

The operator is not liable for any damage arising from the use of the Site. See the terms of use for details.

6. Contact

For questions about this policy, please contact X (@hotaka_1123).

7. Changes

This policy may be revised without notice when necessary. Revisions take effect when they are posted on this page.
