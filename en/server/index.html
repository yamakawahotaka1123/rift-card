Make a Discord server card

Make a free server card to recruit members for your League of Legends Discord server. Add the server name, rank range, tags and invite link, and you get an image with a champion skin background. Post it on X or on server listing sites.

How to use

  1. Fill in the basics
    Enter the server name, icon, member count, active hours and invite link. Pick the bottom and top of your rank range to show something like “Silver – Diamond” with rank emblems. Leave both on “Any” to show “All ranks welcome”.
  2. Describe the server and pick tags
    Say what kind of server it is and choose the tags that fit. Add anything missing under “Other tags”, separated by commas.
  3. Choose a theme and background, then save
    All summoner card themes are available. Use a champion skin or your own image (such as your server banner) as the background.

What you enter is saved only in this browser and never sent to a server.

Tips for a card that brings people in

  • Sum up the vibe: open the description with who will enjoy the server, such as “serious ranked grinding” or “relaxed games and chat”.
  • List active hours: knowing when people are around helps newcomers tell whether they’ll have someone to play with.
  • Use a non-expiring invite: Discord invites expire after 7 days unless you choose “Never” when creating them. Use a permanent link on the image.
  • Keep the rules separate: put the appeal on the card and the detailed rules in a rules channel inside the server.
