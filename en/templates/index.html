Summoner Card Templates (Themes)

SUMMONER CARD has 15 templates (themes) to choose from. There’s no image editing involved: fill in the fields and your card is done. Press “Use this template” under a sample to open the maker with that theme selected.

Every theme can use any champion skin or your own uploaded image as the background. Just changing the background gives the card a whole new feel.
