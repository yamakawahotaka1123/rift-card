Promo banners (free to use)

These banners are for anyone kind enough to introduce SUMMONER CARD. Put them on your stream overlay, Twitch panels, YouTube description, blog or website — no need to ask. Where you can add a link, linking to https://summonercard.com/en/ would be much appreciated.

No need to ask, but if you use one, a quick hello on X (@hotaka_1123) would make my day 🙏 I’d love to check out your stream or post!

Make a banner with your own card

Press “🎨 Make a promo banner with your card” in the card maker to get a banner (1800×600) featuring the card you made. It works as a Twitch panel image or an X header too. Cards with a dreamy theme get a pastel banner.

Banners to download (all themes)

Click an image to open it full size (1800×600) and save it from there.

How to add one

Blog or website

Paste this HTML as is.

<a href="https://summonercard.com/en/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><img src="https://summonercard.com/img/en/banner.jpg" alt="SUMMONER CARD | Summoner card maker" width="600" height="200"></a>

For another theme, change banner.jpg to that image’s file name (for example banner-halloween.jpg).

Twitch panel

On your channel, go to “About” → “Edit panels”, upload the banner as the image and put https://summonercard.com/en/ in “Image links to”.

As text

Make your LoL summoner card here👉 https://summonercard.com/en/

Please note

  • Feel free to resize or crop the banners.
  • Please don’t sell the banners or use them in a way that suggests you’re the official SUMMONER CARD.
  • The banners contain LoL artwork. See the terms of use for Riot Games’ rights.
