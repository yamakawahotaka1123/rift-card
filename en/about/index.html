About SUMMONER CARD

SUMMONER CARD is a free, individually run tool made so League of Legends players can easily create “summoner cards” to introduce themselves and find friends. The goal is that you can make a card just by filling in fields, without editing a template image.

Contact

Bug reports, feature requests or anything about the site: feel free to send a DM or reply on X (@hotaka_1123). The operator is based in Japan, but messages in English are welcome. For bugs, it helps a lot to mention your device and browser (for example, Safari on iPhone, or inside the X app) and what you were doing when it happened.

Assets used

  • Champion, skin and icon images: Riot Games “Data Dragon”
  • Rank emblems, role icons and summoner icons: Community Dragon
  • Fonts: Google Fonts (M PLUS 1p, M PLUS Rounded 1c, Cinzel, Dancing Script, Noto Sans)
