FAQ

What is a summoner card?
It’s a profile image where League of Legends players sum up their rank, roles, main champions, play times and so on. People use them to find duo partners and friends on X, Discord servers and elsewhere.
Is it free? Do I need an account?
It’s completely free, and there’s no sign-up or login.
Can I make one on my phone?
Yes, right in your phone’s browser. On iPhone, pressing “Save image” shows the card full size; long-press it to save it to Photos.
Saving doesn’t work when I open the site from the X app
In-app browsers (X, Instagram, Discord and others) often block saving and sharing images. Choose “Open in browser” from the “…” menu at the top right to open the site in Safari or Chrome. You can also long-press the image that appears to save it.
Do I need to edit a template image?
No image editing app needed. Fill in the fields and pick a theme, and the card is generated with champion and rank icons.
What if I don’t want to show my summoner name?
Under the summoner name field you can pick a preset instead: “Private”, “Ask when we play”, “Ask me in DMs” or “Once we get along”.
Is my rank fetched automatically?
Not at the moment; you choose it yourself. Unranked players, or anyone who’d rather not show their rank, can use it as is.
Is anything I enter sent anywhere?
No. What you enter and the images you upload are processed and stored only in your browser and are never sent to our server.
Can I edit my card later?
Your input is saved automatically in your browser, so you can pick up where you left off next time. It won’t carry over if you clear your browser data or open the site on another device or browser.
Can I use the images however I like?
You can freely post them on X, Discord, your stream, Twitch panels and so on. You can’t sell the images or the LoL artwork in them. See the terms of use for details.
Can I remove the URL in the bottom right of the card?
Yes: untick “Add the site URL to the bottom right of the card” below the design section. If you leave it on, it really helps the site grow!
